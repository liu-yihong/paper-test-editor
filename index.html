Who’s behind this? This page is a side project by Yihong Liu, an instructor who can watch every screen in the computer lab but not the one on a student’s own laptop, and so built an entire website to bring back the most advanced exam security technology known: paper. It runs on free hosting, strong tea and a quiet conviction that no two make-up forms should ever share a question. Your question banks never leave your browser: not to Yihong, not to GitHub, and certainly not to whoever is very curious about what’s on Form B.
